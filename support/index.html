How to play

Your phone is the mouth. Fruit falls down the fold; fold the phone shut to bite it. Never bite a cactus — let it fall. A gentle fold is enough; there is no need to snap the phone hard.

Not on an iPhone Duo? Tap anywhere to bite.

Pass the Duo (party mode)

2–4 players share one phone. Everyone gets one life; pass the phone after your turn. Highest score wins.

Common questions

The bite feels late or early. Fold past about a third of the way closed to bite, then open again before the next bite. Let us know your device and how it felt — we tune this from your feedback.

No sound? Gulpo follows your silent switch and plays alongside your own music.

Did I lose my best score? It is stored only on your phone, so deleting the app resets it.
