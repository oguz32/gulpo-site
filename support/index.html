How to play

Put the phone flat on a table between two players. Each player uses their own half of the screen — that end is your goal, your paddle, your court.

Works on any iPhone. On bigger or unfolding screens the table simply gets bigger.

The games

Gulpo Hockey — air hockey: drag your paddle and bank the puck into the goal. First to 7.
Gulpo Soccer — beat the goalkeeper and score. 90 seconds or 5 goals.
Gulpo Tennis — move your racket, angle the ball past your rival. 7 points, win by 2.

Common questions

A touch did not register. Keep your fingers on your own half of the screen, away from the very top and bottom edges (those belong to the system). If it still happens, write to us with your device model.

No sound? Gulpo follows your silent switch and plays alongside your own music.
