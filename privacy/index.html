Privacy Policy

Gulpo games (Gulpo Hockey, Gulpo Soccer, Gulpo Tennis) do not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Nothing. The games do not save any personal information on your device.

Sensors

Gulpo games are played with touch only. They do not use the camera, microphone, location, motion or hinge sensors.

Sharing

When you tap Share on the result screen, the game creates an image of the final score and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it.

Network

The game works fully offline. It does not connect to our servers or to any third-party service.

Children

Gulpo collects no data from anyone, including children.
