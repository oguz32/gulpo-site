Privacy Policy

Gulpo: Fold to Bite does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Two small settings are saved only on your device: your best score and whether you have finished the tutorial. They never leave your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

Hinge and sensors

On iPhone Duo, Gulpo reads the hinge angle to know when you fold the phone. The angle is used live, in the game only. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you tap Share Score or Share Result, Gulpo creates an image of your score and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it.

Network

The game works fully offline. It does not connect to our servers or to any third-party service.

Children

Gulpo collects no data from anyone, including children.
